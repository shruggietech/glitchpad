Logo system

Marks and lockups

The paper and G share one construction. The G is cut from the page. The sulfur corner makes Glitchpad easy to identify in app lists, tabs, and file association menus.

Clear space

One clear-space unit on every side: 70 units on the 800 × 1000 canvas, 9.7 percent of artwork width. No text, border, icon or crop enters that band.

Below 32 px the reduced master takes over. It ships as its own file. Do not rasterise the full mark down at runtime.

Prohibited

No rotation, skew, stretch, outline, bevel or glow. Never recolour individual elements. Never set the wordmark in live text or a substitute typeface. Never combine the glitchpad and ShruggieTech marks into one lockup.

Light surfaces

The bright accent #FFD900 measures 1.3:1 on the light reading surface and is never text there. The light token block substitutes #867100 at 4.51:1 automatically. The legal foreground on an accent fill is #000000 at 15.18:1. Every fill token in brand.json carries its measured foreground.

Colour vision

The emphasis orange and the failure red sit close in hue and are not reliably separable under deuteranopia. That is acceptable only because state is never carried by colour alone: every state ships a written label.

Rules of use

Series order is fixed: chart-1 is always the primary measurement. Never reorder to make a chart look better. Never introduce a sixth colour; past five series, switch to a form that does not depend on hue. A series never uses the emphasis orange or the failure red, because those carry state.

Available weights

Geist ships 400 and 500. Geist Mono ships 400 only. Space Grotesk ships 500 and 700. Any other weight makes the renderer synthesise a faux bold, which prints badly and forces outlined glyphs into exported PDFs. In mono, carry emphasis with colour.
