# Glitchpad concept exploration, round 04

These six generated images are geometry studies based on the round 03 File Intake rendition. They are ideation references only. Color was intentionally held constant and remains provisional. Any selected direction must be reconstructed as hand-authored filled-path SVG geometry on a declared square grid.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Glitchpad logo-mark variation, geometry study
Input images: Image 1 is the approved direction reference and must remain recognizable
Style/medium: clean flat vector-style logo exploration, filled geometric shapes, professional technical identity, minimalist retro-futurist cyberpunk restraint
Composition/framing: exactly one large centered mark on a plain near-black square background with generous margin
Color palette: provisional and constant for geometry comparison: smoked titanium structure, near-black negative space, exactly one sulfur-phosphor yellow fragment; no white, cyan, magenta, purple, blue, or orange
Lighting/mood: silent, controlled, forensic, covert instrument
Constraints: preserve the file-viewer silhouette, folded upper-right corner, fragmented digital bands, and one active anomaly; strong silhouette; no text, numbers, labels, extra marks, gradients, glow, blur, shadows, texture, 3D, mockup, watermark, skull, weapon, shield, eye, crosshair, or decorative background
```

## Variation prompts

### 01. Closed Frame

```text
Primary request: Variation 01, Closed Frame. Refine Image 1 into a more complete document frame with a continuous bottom and right edge, a compact folded upper-right corner, and a controlled opening only on the upper-left. Arrange three horizontal titanium data bands inside the frame with one small titanium square and one sulfur square occupying a deliberate gap. Keep the geometry balanced and authoritative.
```

### 02. Open Intake

```text
Primary request: Variation 02, Open Intake. Make the left edge of the document frame an intentional intake aperture. Three horizontal data bands should enter through that open edge and become progressively more aligned as they move into the pane. Retain the folded upper-right corner and place the single sulfur square at the point where the middle band becomes structured.
```

### 03. Ghost Cell

```text
Primary request: Variation 03, Ghost Cell. Keep a strong closed document outline with the folded upper-right corner. Place three disciplined horizontal data bands inside, but remove one square segment from the central band to form a crisp ghost cell. Position the sulfur square immediately adjacent to that absence as if it has been discovered or extracted. Use maximum negative-space clarity.
```

### 04. Staggered Scan

```text
Primary request: Variation 04, Staggered Scan. Inside the document frame, use four horizontal data bands of intentionally different lengths that step inward toward a common reading axis. Add one small titanium square between bands and make the sulfur square the endpoint of the shortest band. Keep the fold small and the frame rugged but minimal.
```

### 05. Fold Channel

```text
Primary request: Variation 05, Fold Channel. Make the folded upper-right corner part of the information-flow story: a dark diagonal channel should descend subtly from the fold into the document interior and intersect three horizontal data bands. Place the single sulfur square at the intersection. Preserve the document silhouette and keep the diagonal secondary, not dominant.
```

### 06. Reduced Intake

```text
Primary request: Variation 06, Reduced Intake. Design the simplest favicon-oriented version of Image 1. Retain only a bold document frame with a minimal folded corner, two horizontal data bands, one small titanium square, and one sulfur square. Remove every nonessential element while preserving the feeling of digital fragments being organized inside a viewer.
```
